When we run command - "python app.py" and receive a link such as [http://0.0.0.0:5000]

If your app isn’t running at http://0.0.0.0:5000, try Alternatives:

1. Localhost (Default for Flask)
👉 [http://127.0.0.1:5000]
-  This is the safest and most common way to access your Flask app on your local machine.

2. Explicit Localhost Address
👉 [http://localhost:5000]
-  Some systems resolve localhost better than 127.0.0.1.
